# Research
My work has primarily focused on (1) prediction across timescales ranging from subseasonal to multi-year and (2) biogeochemical impacts of extreme events and their effects on marine ecosystems.

### Current work
- <em>Understanding changes to the North Pacific Current and Bifurcation</em> I am contributing to assessments focused on understanding changes to the North Pacific Current modes under anthropogenic warming using a variety of model and observational tools.
- <em>Forecasting extremes in marine productivity</em>. As the global ocean experiences physical and biogeochemical changes as a result of climate change, there are growing concerns about impacts on marine organisms and ecosystems. Here, I use the Community Earth System Model (CESM) Seasonal-to-Multiyear Large Ensemble (SMYLE) alongside observational products and model reconstructions to assess predictions of extremes in marine Chlorophyll, Net Primary Productivity, and Total Biomass. 

### Previous work
- <em>Forecasting marine extreme events</em>. I leveraged the CESM SMYLE to investigate potential to generate forecasts of marine extreme events, including marine heawaves and ocean acidification extremes. I found high and long-lasting skill that is influenced by ENSO state. Read the paper here: <a href="doi.org/10.1038/s41561-024-01593-0"> Multi-month forecasts of marine heatwaves and ocean acidification extremes</a>
- <em>Forecasting surface and subsurface marine ecosystem stressors</em>. I leveraged the CESM SMYLE to investigate potential to generate forecasts of both surface and subsurface marine ecosystem stressors using novel observational products. I found that skillful forecasts are generated in a variety of key marine ecosystems. Read the paper here: <a href="https://doi.org/10.1029/2021gl096938"> Skillful Multi‐Month Predictions of Ecosystem Stressors in the Surface and Subsurface Ocean</a>
- <em>Determining the biogeochemical impacts of the Blob</em>. I leveraged a variety of model and observational datasets to determine the impacts of a regional, long-lasting marine heatwaves on biogeochemical tracers. I determined that the Blob led to a temporary mitigation of acidification and worsening of deoxygenation. Read the paper here: <a href="https://doi.org/10.1029/2021gl096938"> Ocean Biogeochemical Signatures of the North Pacific Blob</a>
- I contributed to the description paper for the Community Earth System Model (CESM) Seasonal-to-Multiyear Large Ensemble (SMYLE), analyzing model biogeochemical performance. Read the paper here: <a href="https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-15-6451-2022"> The Seasonal-to-Multiyear Large Ensemble (SMYLE) prediction system using the Community Earth System Model version 2</a>
